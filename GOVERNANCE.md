# Governance

Open Flow is currently maintained by its founding maintainer. Governance should become more distributed as the contributor community grows.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- Protect contributor and subject privacy
- Review pull requests and policy reports fairly
- Keep schemas and documentation understandable
- Explain major technical or policy decisions
- Avoid using project access for personal disputes
- Disclose meaningful conflicts of interest

## Decisions

Routine decisions can be made through pull request review. Major changes to licensing, privacy rules, governance, or dataset structure should be proposed in a public issue or discussion before merging when no urgent safety problem exists.

Privacy, security, and legal-risk removals may happen immediately. The public explanation should avoid repeating sensitive information.

## Becoming a maintainer

Consistent contributors may be invited to maintain specific areas. Access should follow the least-privilege principle and may be removed if it is unused or misused.

## Conflicts

A maintainer should step back from a decision when they have a personal, financial, or organizational conflict that could affect impartial review.
