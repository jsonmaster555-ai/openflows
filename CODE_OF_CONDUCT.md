# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Open Flow should be a safe, useful, and respectful community. Contributors, maintainers, and participants must:

- Treat people with respect.
- Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy, consent, and personal boundaries.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Correct mistakes without harassing the person who made them.
- Follow the submission and content policies.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted insults
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Publishing or threatening to publish personal information
- Encouraging privacy violations or unauthorized access
- Deliberately submitting unsafe, illegal, or infringing content
- Spam, impersonation, coordinated disruption, or repeated bad-faith behavior

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, restrict participation, or ban accounts when needed to protect the project and its community.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct problems, open a moderation issue without including private information. For reports involving sensitive personal data, security issues, threats, or material that should not be public, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

All reports should be handled as privately and fairly as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
