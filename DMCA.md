# Copyright and Removal Requests

Open Flow respects intellectual property rights and accepts good-faith requests to review or remove material.

## Before sending a request

Identify the exact repository path or website URL at issue. General requests to remove an entire product name or category may be difficult to evaluate without specific locations.

## Include in a copyright request

- Your full legal name and preferred contact information
- Identification of the copyrighted work
- The exact Open Flow URL or repository path containing the material
- A short explanation of why the use is unauthorized
- A statement that you have a good-faith belief the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
- A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act for the owner
- Your physical or electronic signature

Send the request to the official contact address listed by the repository maintainers. Do not publish private contact information in a GitHub issue.

## Other removal reasons

For privacy, trademark, safety, or confidential-information concerns, identify the material and explain the concern. Use the private security route for exposed personal data or secrets.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers may temporarily hide or remove material while reviewing a credible request. They may ask for clarification, preserve a limited internal record, restore material when appropriate, or follow a platform-specific legal process.

This document is a project process, not legal advice and not a substitute for any procedure required by applicable law or GitHub.
