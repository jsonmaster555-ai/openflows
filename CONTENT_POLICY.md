# Content Policy

This policy explains what Open Flow accepts, restricts, and removes.

## Allowed content

Open Flow may accept sanitized interface captures that are useful for design reference, documentation, research, commentary, or education, including:

- Individual app and website screens
- Complete user flows
- Public product pages and public interfaces
- Interfaces captured from accounts the contributor is authorized to use
- Test, demo, sandbox, or empty-account interfaces
- Accurate metadata and interface version information

## Restricted content

The following requires careful review and may be rejected:

- Interfaces containing user-generated content
- Dating, social, messaging, school, workplace, finance, health, or location interfaces
- Paid or account-only interfaces
- Screens that could expose a person's identity even after basic redaction
- Content involving children or other vulnerable people
- Content containing third-party media unrelated to explaining the interface

Maintainers may require stronger redaction or decline a submission without publishing it.

## Prohibited content

Do not submit:

- Unblurred recognizable people or personal profile photos
- Personal data, private messages, credentials, secrets, or financial information
- Sexual content or intimate imagery
- Exploitative or abusive material
- Leaked, stolen, confidential, or unlawfully accessed material
- Content obtained by bypassing access controls or technical protections
- Malware, malicious links, or instructions intended to compromise systems
- Material that clearly infringes rights without a credible reference, commentary, documentation, or educational purpose
- Deceptive edits presented as authentic screenshots
- Spam, ads submitted as contributions, or unrelated media

## Third-party rights

Open Flow does not claim ownership of third-party apps, interfaces, screenshots, logos, product names, or trademarks. Rights remain with their respective owners.

The AGPL-3.0 license applies to Open Flow source code, not automatically to third-party media in the dataset. Contributors must not apply an open-source license to material they do not own.

## Removal and moderation

Maintainers may blur, restrict, label, or remove content that:

- Violates this policy
- Creates a privacy or safety risk
- Is duplicate, misleading, corrupted, or too low quality
- Receives a credible rights-holder complaint
- Creates unreasonable legal or operational risk for the project

Urgent privacy or security problems should be reported privately using [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Copyright requests should follow [DMCA.md](DMCA.md).

## Repeat violations

Repeated or intentional violations can result in contribution restrictions or a community ban. Serious privacy or security violations may be handled immediately without prior warning.

## Policy changes

This policy may change as the project grows. The version in the default branch is the current policy.
